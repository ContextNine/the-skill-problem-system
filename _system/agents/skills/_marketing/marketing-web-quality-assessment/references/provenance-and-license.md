# Provenance and License

This skill incorporates and adapts the six skills and three reference documents from Addy Osmani's [`web-quality-skills`](https://github.com/addyosmani/web-quality-skills) repository at commit `95d6e255afe1596b557d7a8498517884438f5b3a`:

- `accessibility`, including its WCAG and accessibility-pattern references
- `best-practices`
- `core-web-vitals`, including its LCP reference
- `performance`
- `seo`
- `web-quality-audit`

The source frontmatter was removed, internal links were adapted for the consolidated layout, and executable Lighthouse, PageSpeed Insights, summarization, and CI guidance was added.
