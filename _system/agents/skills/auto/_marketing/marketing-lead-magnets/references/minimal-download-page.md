# Minimal download page

Use this variant when delivery and one concise next step are enough. Keep it deliberately small:

1. `Thanks for downloading <resource>` confirmation.
2. One obvious download button with filename and format context.
3. An optional relevant meme or lightweight visual.
4. One or two sentences connecting the resource to the next offer.
5. One primary upsell CTA and at most one quiet secondary link.

The download must remain the dominant action and work without playing media. Do not add proof strips, multiple testimonial sections, a founder story, long problem agitation, repeated CTAs, or FAQ; choosing those means the page has become a full VSL and should be designed as one intentionally.

Verify mobile layout, keyboard focus, link purpose, asset filename/type/size context, download analytics, upsell attribution, and a graceful response when the asset is temporarily unavailable.
