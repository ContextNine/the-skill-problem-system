# Full VSL page

Use a long-form post-opt-in page only when the offer has enough truthful copy and proof. The audited KubeCraft example used confirmation, video, recognizable proof, testimonials, problem and belief-breaking sections, program mechanism, founder story, more proof, steps, FAQ, and repeated application calls to action.

## Flexible sequence

1. Confirm delivery and provide an immediate download near the top.
2. State the next outcome in a large heading with short bridge copy.
3. Use a prominent video poster/player with an obvious play action and no autoplay audio.
4. Add a compact authority or recognizable-proof strip.
5. Show relevant testimonials and transformation stories.
6. Articulate the problem and challenge key mistaken beliefs.
7. Explain the mechanism, offer, or next step.
8. Add the founder/practitioner story and further proof.
9. Repeat one restrained primary CTA at genuine decision points.
10. Finish with FAQ, scope/boundary copy, and a final CTA.

Every section is optional. Remove it when proof, copy, or the offer does not justify it. Do not turn logos into implied endorsements or turn anecdotal outcomes into guarantees.

## Quality checks

- Keep the download action visible without forcing the visitor through the VSL.
- Make headings, video controls, transcript/captions, focus order, contrast, and CTA labels accessible.
- Use a responsive poster, lazy-load below-the-fold media, reserve layout space, and avoid blocking third-party embeds.
- Respect reduced motion. Never autoplay audio; provide captions or a transcript.
- Track page view, download, video start/progress/complete, primary CTA, and application start with stable offer IDs.
- Verify small-screen reading order, vertical scrolling, Core Web Vitals, privacy/consent boundaries, and outbound attribution.
