# Competitor research and briefs

These conventions apply when the resolved repository already uses this competitor artifact layout. All paths below are relative to that repository root. Otherwise use its own conventions.

- Dossier: `./.agents/blog/competitors/research/<slug>.md`.
- Comparison brief: `./.agents/blog/competitors/blogs/<slug>-vs-impression.md`.
- Alternatives brief: `./.agents/blog/competitors/blogs/best-<slug>-alternatives.md`.
- Product context: `./.docs/exported-feature-descriptions.md`, relevant `./.docs/DMs-and-outreach.md` and `./.docs/auto-dm-auto-reply-auto-plug.md`, and implementation evidence when a claim is uncertain.

Reuse existing slugs, dossiers, and briefs. Research may cover several requested competitors, but create both article briefs only when requested or when the selected opportunity needs both. An ordinary how-to article does not require competitor artifacts.

## Dossier

Keep the official site, category, best-fit customer, promise, current pricing and source confidence, product notes, outside-market framing, comparison notes, caveats, and dated source log. Separate official claims from independent experiences. Favor a smaller set of strong sources over a target source count.

## Comparison brief

Include search intent and an honest thesis; competitor and owner snapshots; applicable comparison axes; current pricing/packaging; when to choose each product; category boundaries; coming-soon claims; caveats; and claims to avoid. Put the facts and supporting links directly into the brief so the writer does not need to reconstruct them from a dossier.

## Alternatives brief

Include why users leave the competitor; the fair candidate set and selection criteria; where the owning product belongs or does not belong; an earned ranking; current product/pricing context; switching constraints; caveats; and claims to avoid. Do not claim full replacement for a partially overlapping product.

Comparison and alternatives conclusions must agree. Verify changing facts when producing the brief, even if an older dossier looks complete. Preserve unfavorable evidence and disclose uncertainty rather than silently remove it.
