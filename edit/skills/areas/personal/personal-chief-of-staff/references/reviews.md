# Daily and weekly reviews

Review questions go to Matt in chat. Do not write answers, completion claims, or next-week goals before he replies, unless the relevant note already contains an answered review. Do not ask again while a questionnaire is pending in this conversation. When only some answers exist, ask only what is missing and useful.

## Daily

Ask what he completed today, what went well, what he learned, and what did not go well. After answers arrive, put their useful substance under semantically suitable existing headings in today's personal daily note. Check work off only when his answer or another reliable completion record identifies the completed action. Use the answers as evidence to update broad goals in the current personal weekly note. Do not convert a vague win into a quantified Commitment.

## Sunday weekly

Ask about this week's goals, completed work, what worked, what he learned, and what did not work. After answers arrive, write the review under suitable existing headings in the current personal weekly note. Mark a broad goal complete only when the answers or checked source work establish the whole goal. Then read `./planning.md` and write next week's broad goals into the next personal weekly source note using next week's actual capacity. If it does not exist, generate it with `vault periodic --date <next-Monday> --teamspace-folders personal` and verify that you edit the personal weekly source, not a rollup.

Return a short status once the notes are updated. Do not present the written next-week goals as a separate proposal in chat.
